Two ways to partner. Both earn recurring revenue.

POST /v1/transcribe

Real-time and batch speech-to-text with accent profile selection. <500ms latency.

Semantic extraction: topics, action items, entities, sentiment. Mixed-language native.

Formatted deliverables: meeting notes, compliance reports, .srt subtitles, custom templates.
